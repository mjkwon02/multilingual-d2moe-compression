## Table 2 (revised, full)

SIB-200 topic-classification accuracy (%) after compression, full test set (n = 204 per language; 14-shot, fixed English demonstrations). Cells for compressed models are mean ± sample standard deviation (ddof = 1) over 3 calibration seeds, in percentage points; the Uncompressed row is a single evaluation and carries no standard deviation. All seeds, including collapsed ones, enter the mean and standard deviation. [k/3] marks the number of seeds whose predictions collapsed (top-label share > 0.9 or accuracy < 25%); cells without a bracket have no collapsed seed. The 25% threshold is the project's pre-existing collapse criterion and is not the 7-way uniform-chance rate (1/7 ≈ 14.3%). Unlike the submitted version, which replaced fully collapsed cells with a placeholder, raw accuracy is now reported for every cell and collapse is marked separately. Bold marks, within each evaluation-language column, the higher mean of the own-language calibration and Balanced-character calibration; it is a descriptive comparison of means, not a significance test. Values are the originally reported runs; retention relative to the uncompressed model is given separately in the retention table.

## Table 2-clean

SIB-200 topic-classification accuracy (%) on the clean subset (n = 98 per language; the same 98 items in all five languages and for all models; same prompt and 14-shot demonstrations as the full evaluation). The Uncompressed row is the uncompressed model evaluated on this same subset. Cells for compressed models are mean ± sample standard deviation (ddof = 1) over 3 calibration seeds, in percentage points; the Uncompressed row is a single evaluation and carries no standard deviation. All seeds, including collapsed ones, enter the mean and standard deviation. [k/3] marks the number of seeds whose predictions collapsed (top-label share > 0.9 or accuracy < 25%); cells without a bracket have no collapsed seed. The 25% threshold is the project's pre-existing collapse criterion and is not the 7-way uniform-chance rate (1/7 ≈ 14.3%). Unlike the submitted version, which replaced fully collapsed cells with a placeholder, raw accuracy is now reported for every cell and collapse is marked separately. Bold marks, within each evaluation-language column, the higher mean of the own-language calibration and Balanced-character calibration; it is a descriptive comparison of means, not a significance test. Pending: fewer than 3 seeds completed; no mean or bold is shown for such cells, and bold is shown only where both compared conditions have all 3 seeds. Clean and full accuracies are measured on different item sets, so their difference is descriptive only.

## Supplementary retention table

SIB-200 retention (ratio, ×; not a percentage), defined as compressed-model accuracy divided by uncompressed accuracy on the same evaluation subset: the full-test baseline (n = 204) for the full panel and the clean-subset baseline (n = 98) for the clean panel. Mean ± sample standard deviation (ddof = 1) of per-seed retention over 3 seeds, collapsed seeds included; [k/3] as in the accuracy tables. A high retention in a cell with collapsed seeds, or where the uncompressed accuracy is itself low (e.g. SW), should not be read as preserved task ability. Pending: fewer than 3 seeds completed.
